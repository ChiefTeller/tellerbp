Zuga Theory תורת זוגא

Zuga is the infinite movement between a pair of phenomena between which there are relations of opposition, complementarity, dependence, independence and mutual influence. Between them a range of possibilities is spread, and the tension between them changes. The very infinite movement and the change of the tension create infinite possibilities that are spread across the range and sustain Zuga.

According to the mathematical ontological structure of Zuga, every action, movement, claim, definition and even reference to Zuga exist within Zuga and sustain it. This is an infinite framework that also describes itself. In the simplest words, Zuga can be defined as the Theory of Everything.
